# Where each paper's data lives

Generated by `scripts/run_data_availability.py`. Per paper, `<SDY>_<PMID>_llm.json` has the quotes and the LLM's reason behind every cell; `<SDY>_<PMID>.json` has the validated data repositories and their links.

- **LLM**: whether the LLM answered. Its answer decides every role; every disagreement with the DA statement or the repository record is a REVIEW line. Without an answer, the paper's IDs are **Unclear**. **DA statement**: how the paper's Data Availability statement was found (*section* / *inline_label*), or *none*.
- **Deposited**: this paper's own data. **Reused**: another study's data this paper analysed. **Referenced**: named but not used. **Unclear**: needs a human look (see the REVIEW lines in the JSON).
