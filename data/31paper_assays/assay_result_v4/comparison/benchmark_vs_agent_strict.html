A benchmark row counts as matched once the scorer pairs it with an agent entry on any tier, whether or not the two agree on a term: MATCH (14) + MATCH GRANULARITY (0) + MATCH OBI CONFLICT (1) + MATCH OBI UNMAPPED (0) = 15. Only MISSED, a row no tier could pair, lowers it.

OBI recall = ontology-paired ÷ benchmark assays = (14 + 0) / 16 = 14 / 16 = 87.5%
Only a pairing the ontology itself made counts: the same term (MATCH), or one term a parent or child of the other (MATCH GRANULARITY). A row paired by a shared name or quote carries a different or missing term, so CONFLICT and UNMAPPED count toward recall and not here — that gap is the 1 row between the two tiles.

Strict mode: both denominators are the 16 rows the curators wrote. A curated row left without a partner is MISSED even when another row on the same term was paired, and the 0 pairs the ontology alone made were taken only after every pair the names made.
