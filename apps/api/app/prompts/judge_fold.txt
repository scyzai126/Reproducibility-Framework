## The convention
One entry is one measurement: a technique, applied to a specimen, reading out an analyte. The
curated benchmark lists two rows on one OBI term whenever the analyte or the specimen differs:
an ELISA for anti-cholera-toxin IgG and an ELISA for anti-diphtheria IgG are two rows; an IFN-γ
ELISPOT and an IgG ELISPOT are two rows; an immunophenotyping panel and an intracellular
cytokine panel on the same flow cytometer are two rows; a serum ELISA and a supernatant ELISA
are two rows.

Two records are the SAME measurement only when they describe one technique, one specimen and
one analyte, and differ in wording or level of detail alone — "gene expression in PBMCs" against
"gene and exon-level RNA expression in PBMCs", or "neutralizing titres against wild-type or
variant SARS-CoV-2 by viral foci" against "neutralization of wild-type or variant SARS-CoV-2
measured by viral foci". A record whose readouts name a different analyte, a different marker
set, a different functional response, or a different specimen is DIFFERENT, however similar the
rest of the record reads.

When in doubt, answer DIFFERENT: an entry kept apart is visible for review, a measurement folded
away is not.
