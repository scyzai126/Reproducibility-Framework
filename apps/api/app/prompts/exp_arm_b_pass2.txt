## PASS 2 — draft + legend, reconciliation

You are in PASS 2. You receive your PASS 1 draft claims (written from the Results prose alone) and, for
the first time, the **figure legend** for this panel. The Results prose is still available to you.

Work through this checklist in order:

1. **Resolve `unspecified`.** For each PASS 1 claim, use the legend to fill any field left `null` —
   cohort size, statistical test, sample type, what each sub-panel plots. Drop each item from
   `unspecified` once the legend resolves it; keep the ones it does not.

2. **Correct the panel assignment.** The legend says what each sub-panel actually shows. Move a claim to
   the right sub-panel where PASS 1 guessed wrong, and split a claim that PASS 1 attached to a whole
   figure when the legend shows its assertions live in different sub-panels.

3. **Correct scope.** Where the legend makes a target, group or timepoint more specific than the prose
   did ("IgG" → "total IgG"; "both vaccines" → "n=13 MPSV4 and n=17 MCV4"), tighten the claim. Record each
   such change in `scope_changes`.

4. **Add legend-only claims.** A legend sometimes states a finding in words that the prose never does
   (e.g. "a strong IgG response against diphtheria toxoid was detected in MCV4 vaccinees"). That counts as
   the authors stating it — add it as a new claim with `"from_legend": true`.

5. **Re-apply the split rules.** With the legend's extra precision, some PASS 1 claims turn out to chain
   two assertions, or two turn out to be one. Re-run the split/merge rules over the reconciled set.

6. **Demote what the sources do not support.** Drop any claim whose `quote` you cannot now locate verbatim
   in either the prose or the legend.

The legend is evidence for assertions the authors state. It does not license findings that exist only as
plotted data.
