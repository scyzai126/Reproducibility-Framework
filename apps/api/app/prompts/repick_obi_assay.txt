## The question

The earlier pass stopped at a technique-level category. Is there a candidate that is **the same
technique made more specific** -- a child or protocol-level term of that category, not a
different kind of term?

What counts as more specific, within the technique:

- A named functional protocol run on that instrument: an activation-induced marker (AIM)
  assay, a proliferation-dye or CFSE assay, ELISPOT, a hemagglutination inhibition assay, a
  plaque-reduction or focus-reduction neutralization test, MHC multimer/tetramer staining.
- A qualifier the record states: single-cell or bulk for sequencing, reverse-transcription or
  real-time for PCR, bead-based multiplex rather than a single-analyte plate assay.

Choose such a candidate only when the record's name, aliases or readouts state the thing that
makes it specific. A term naming a reagent ("... using carboxyfluorescein succinimidyl ester
staining") fits only when the record names that reagent.

**Never choose a term that classifies by analyte or purpose** -- "cytokine assay", "analyte
assay", "immune response assay", any "assay detecting ..." or "assay measuring ..." term.
The readouts already record what was measured; such a term says nothing about how the
measurement was made, and it is not a more specific form of the technique the earlier pass
chose. A category term of another kind ("cytometry assay", "immunoassay") is no answer either.

Multiplex immunoassays, the same rule the first pass follows: one sample assayed for many
analytes at once on beads -- "multiplex", a panel of dozens of cytokines or chemokines, Luminex,
xMAP, Bio-Plex, Milliplex, a "... Plex Panel", a "multiplex ELISA" -- is "cytometric bead array
assay"; the beads read on a flow cytometer (LEGENDplex, BD CBA, "beads analyzed by flow
cytometry") is "analyte detection by flow based bead assay"; Olink is "Olink assay" /
"proximity extension assay"; Meso Scale Discovery (MSD, ECL) is "electrochemiluminescence
assay". The "antigen detection by ..." child adds nothing for a cytokine panel, and a plate
assay measuring one analyte per well stays "enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay".

Synonyms are equal to labels.

If no candidate is the same technique made more specific, output exactly "none". The record
then keeps its category term, which is a correct if coarse answer, and stays an entry of its own.
