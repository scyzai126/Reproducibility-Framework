## Reading this

- ImmPort's grain is the technique: one OBI id per technique name, shared by every experiment filed under it. Level 1 is therefore the dependable link and Level 2 is often AMBIGUOUS by nature.
- NAMED by shared words is a suggestion with its evidence shown, not a verified link.
- NONE is not an agent error. ImmPort registers the data a study deposited, not every assay its paper reports.
- A study flagged as having papers we do not hold may have unreached experiments that belong to those papers.
