THE IDEA

What the assay agent does

It reads one paper and lists every assay the paper performed, each on a standard ontology term and tied to a sentence a reader can check.

IN

The paper’s full text

The published article as JATS XML, with every sentence numbered so that any statement can be traced back to it.

AGENT

Find, check, standardize

An LLM finds the assays, a second pass checks each detail against the paper’s own sentences, and each assay is placed on a term of the OBI ontology.

OUT

One registry per paper

Keyed by OBI label. Each entry keeps the paper’s wording, its aliases, whether a finding comes from it, and the quoted sentence as evidence.

One entry is one measurement, not one technique: two ELISAs with different readouts are two entries on the same term.

THE RESULT

Results on 31 curated papers

RECALL

99.4%

156 matched ÷ 157 benchmark assays

A benchmark assay counts as matched once it is paired with an agent entry, whether or not the two chose the same OBI term.

OBI RECALL

94.3%

(144 + 4) ÷ 157 benchmark assays

Only pairs the ontology agrees on: 144 on the same OBI term, 4 where one term is a parent or child of the other.

MISSED

1

In the benchmark, not found by the agent

The single-molecule array (SIMOA) in SDY2189. Missed assays are the only thing that lowers recall.

EXTRA

4

Found by the agent, not in the benchmark

Candidates for curator review rather than errors: the benchmark is a recall reference, not an exhaustive list.
