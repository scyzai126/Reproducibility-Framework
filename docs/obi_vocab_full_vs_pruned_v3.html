The three vocabularies nest. full is the whole OBI assay branch. noncompositional removes the 545 IEDB-style labels ("ELISPOT assay measuring epitope specific IFN-γ production by T cells"). pruned, the live pool, also removes 548 out-of-scope domain terms (hospital analyzer panels, entomology, electrophysiology, metrology, …).

Step 0: keep only assays (5218 → 1794)

OBI imports terms from about thirty other ontologies and defines many non-assay classes of its own. Dropped here: planning, study design, data transformation, specimen (OBI process and material classes); insulin, water (CHEBI chemicals); uterine cervix, pituitary gland (UBERON anatomy); primary cultured cell (CL); chromatin (GO). None of these can be the answer to "which assay did the paper run", so none should be a candidate. Four CHMO microscopy and chromatography terms stay because OBI files them under assay.

Step 1: drop the compositional labels (1794 → 1249)

The IEDB contributed hundreds of terms that bake an analyte and a cell type into the assay name: ELISPOT assay measuring epitope specific interferon-gamma production by T cells, cytometric bead array assay measuring epitope specific IP-10 production by T cells, measuring glucose concentration in blood serum assay. The rule is one regex, \b(measuring|detecting)\b, on the label.

Why: these are near-duplicates of a family term (ELISPOT, CBA, flow cytometry) and they outnumber it (43 of the 45 ELISPOT-shaped labels are compositional), so in a top-20 retrieval they bury the term the curators actually use.

Example · SDY1669

"TLR4-based reporter assay" (readout: plasma LPS) is curated to OBI:0003793 bacterial lipopolysaccharide detection in plasma assay. In the 701 list it ranked #18 and was picked. In the 1794 list, 12 of the 20 candidates were compositional, mostly "cell culture analyte detection bioassay measuring epitope specific IL-4 / TNF / IFN-γ … production by T cells". They sit close to "reporter assay" in embedding space and pushed #18 off the list, so the picker fell back to #6, reporter cell line analyte detection bioassay, marked BROAD.

Example · multi-cytokine ICS

An ICS that reads IFN-γ, TNF and IL-4 at once. Each IEDB child names one cytokine, so no child describes the assay; the lack of a more specific term is the vocabulary's, not the picker being cautious. Since a typical vaccine paper's ICS is multi-cytokine, the flow cytometry family (the one with the most compositional children) will almost always stop at the family term.

The A/B below measures the cost of leaving them in: 10 exact. One label is pinned back, OBI:1110179 MHC tetramer/multimer assay measuring binding of a T cell epitope:MHC:TCR complex, because OBI names it as the replacement for the retired tetramer staining term and the family has no plain term left.

Step 2: drop out-of-scope domains (1249 → 701)

These are domains unlikely to be the answer to "which assay did this immunology study run" that still sit close enough in embedding space to act as decoys. The criterion is a judgement per domain, not a check that each term never appears in a paper: tiers A and C are biomedical and clinical papers do report them, and they go because they are not the study's assay. Tiers overlap through multiple inheritance, so the column sums past 548. Why: these terms are semantic near-neighbours of ones we want and the mapper has no similarity threshold to keep them out, so a decoy that reaches the top 20 reaches the LLM.

Tier A is the only one filtered by label rather than wholesale: the clinical band also holds plain analyte readouts a paper does cite (C-reactive protein assay, neutrophil count assay), so only the instrument- and panel-qualified labels go, and each one's plain sibling stays. Two families are whitelisted back because the tier would leave them with nothing: the IgG / IgM / IgA / IgE quantitation terms (OBI has no bare IgG assay), and OBI:0000912 X-ray crystallography.

The only empirical check on the choice is this corpus: the step removed no term any of the 31 papers was curated to, and the A/B below measures it at 1 exact (SDY1293). Rarely decisive, never harmful here.

Why 701 beats 1794

In the full arm, 38% of all candidates shown to the picker were compositional labels; in the pruned arm 0.4%. That causes two failures.

1. A compositional child outranks the family term (7 entries)

The right term is on the list, but the picker takes the construct that names the paper's cytokine or analyte.

  • SDY1364, "IFN-γ ELISpot": pruned picked OBI:0600031 enzyme-linked immunospot assay (#1). The full list had 18 compositional ELISPOTs; the picker took OBI:1110059 ELISPOT assay measuring epitope specific interferon-gamma production by T cells. Curator: OBI:0600031.
  • SDY2189, "Anti-spike binding ELISA": OBI:0000661 ELISA was #1 in both arms; with 13 compositional ELISAs below it, the full arm chose #5, ELISA measuring binding of a B cell epitope:antibody complex.

2. The right term is pushed off the top 20 (6 entries)

Retrieval is 20 slots with no threshold. The curator's term was reachable in the pruned list and absent from the full one.

  • SDY1669, "TLR4-based reporter assay": OBI:0003793 bacterial lipopolysaccharide detection in plasma assay was #18 in the pruned list and was picked. In the full list 12 compositional reporter constructs displaced it; the picker settled for reporter cell line analyte detection bioassay.
  • SDY1289 and SDY1640, cytokine bead arrays: OBI:0003799 analyte detection by flow based bead assay was #4 in the pruned list. In the full list 17 of 20 candidates were "cytometric bead array assay measuring epitope specific …"; it fell off, and the picker took the plain OBI:0000920 cytometric bead array assay.

The one entry the full arm got right is the same mechanism in reverse: SDY1630's NK IFN-γ assay is curated to the compositional OBI:1110207 assay detecting IFN-gamma production, which the pruned pool does not contain, so pruned could only answer flow cytometry assay. One row; the same construct cost the full arm SDY1991.

The full arm also spends 15 more calls: a term that is a leaf in the pruned index has children in the full one (ELISA has 53), so an exact name hit is sent to the picker instead of answered.

Why 701 beats 1249

One entry, SDY1293, "malaria thick blood smears". In the pruned list OBI:0002119 microscopy assay was #8 and was picked. In the 1249 list eight OBI:21xxxxx clinical-panel terms took slots (manual differential bodily fluid macrophage count assay at #1, three CBC with manual differential venous blood … terms further down); microscopy assay was not shown and the picker took blood microbiology assay. The domain prune removes exactly those panel terms.

Recommendation

Keep the 701-term obi_assay_pruned_index.json and keep both prunes. The compositional prune carries the score; the domain prune is cheap and fixes the one case the clinical band can reach. A similarity threshold would not help: the full arm's wrong picks scored as high as its right ones.
