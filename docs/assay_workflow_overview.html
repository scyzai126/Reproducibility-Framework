ImmPort claim reproduction · assay agent · October 2026

From a paper's methods to OBI assay terms

The agent reads one immunology paper and returns every assay the authors performed, each tied to the sentence that describes it and to a term in the Ontology for Biomedical Investigations (OBI). Five rounds turn the paper into that registry: extract, verify, correct, map to OBI, fold. On 31 curated ImmPort papers it finds 156 of 157 curated assays and gives 140 of the 156 matched assays exactly the curator's OBI term.

The whole pipeline

Five rounds, one command

Every round reads the previous round's file and writes its own, so a wrong entry can be traced back to the round that produced it. Every model reply is kept in assays_raw.json.

Round by round

What each round reads, decides and writes

1 · Extract and vote

3 calls
Asks for
Seven fields per assay: name_in_paper, standard_technique_name, aliases, sample_type, readouts, generate_claim, evidence (section + quote).
Derived
source (methods / results) and sentence_ids are computed from the quote by code, never proposed by the model.
Vote
Runs are aligned by name, alias, shared sentence or quote. An assay found by fewer than 2 of 3 runs goes to assays_minority.json and is not delivered.

2 · Verify

1 per assay
Free checks
The quote must appear verbatim in the sentences it cites; every alias must appear in the paper.
Judge
Each field is SUPPORTED, NOT_SUPPORTED or CONTRADICTED by the evidence. A skipped field is NOT_REPORTED.
Verdict
Code, not the model, derives it: identity contradicted → REJECT; any field not supported → REVIEW; otherwise ACCEPT.

3 · Correct

1 per flagged field
Drop
REJECT assays move to assays_rejected.json.
Repair
For each flagged field the model is asked what the paper says, without seeing the old value or the complaint. Result: REAFFIRMED, REPLACED or CLEARED.
Never
The assay's identity is never rewritten. A doubtful identity is marked identity_review in the output instead.

5 · Re-pick and fold

1 per BROAD
Re-pick
A term the picker marked BROAD (a correct category, less specific than the assay) is asked once more with that term removed.
Fold
Two entries merge only when they share the OBI term and agree on technique, sample type and readouts. One entry is one measurement, so two serum neutralization assays on different samples stay apart.
Key
The registry is re-keyed by OBI label; every wording the paper used is kept in name_in_paper.
Round 4 in detail

How one assay reaches an OBI term

The vocabulary is OBI's assay branch, pruned from 1,794 terms to 701. Pruning removed IEDB-style compositional labels ("… assay measuring epitope specific …") and out-of-scope terms, which otherwise crowd the plain family terms out of the search results.

Worked example · SDY180, PMID 23601689

Haemagglutination inhibition, followed through every round

  1. Paper, sentence S136
    "Neutralizing antibody titers were determined as the reciprocal value of the highest dilution that displayed no hemagglutinating activity."

    Section: Hemagglutinin Inhibition and Virus-Neutralization Assays for Quantitating Seasonal Influenza Virus Neutralizing Antibodies.

  2. Round 1 · extract and vote

    name_in_paper "Hemagglutinin Inhibition" · standard_technique_name "hemagglutination inhibition" · sample_type "human sera" · readouts "influenza neutralizing antibody titers" · four aliases, two of them long section headings.

  3. Round 2 · verify

    Identity SUPPORTED. Three of the four aliases NOT_SUPPORTED: they are headings, not names of the assay. Verdict REVIEW.

  4. Round 3 · correct

    Aliases asked again, blind, and REPLACED by "HAI" and "HAI titers".

  5. Round 4 · map to OBI

    No exact name hit. Embedding search puts OBI:0000875 viral hemagglutination inhibition assay first (similarity 0.78); the picker replies "1 exact".

  6. Round 5 · fold, deliver

    Nothing to re-pick or fold. Delivered under the key "viral hemagglutination inhibition assay", which matches the curator's term.

Why it is built this way

Why use an agent instead of one GPT call?One call per paper, 3 runs × 31 papersSame recall, but 14 exact ids (75 if labels are looked up) vs 140; 69 extra entries vs 4Agent
